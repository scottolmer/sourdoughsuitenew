Origin Story

It is not a recipe blog. It is a working bakery tool.

The app is for the moment when a baker has to make a call: wait, fold, cool, proof, bake, scale, or save the formula for tomorrow.
The app covers the full sourdough bench: Photo Rescue, Bake Planner, Baker's Percentage, Starter Log, Recipe Index, and the supporting bench calculators.
Photo Rescue

Quickly triage what the dough is telling you and decide what to do next.

Bake Planner

Back-time a schedule from the bake window, temperature, hydration, and starter strength.

Calculators

Use baker's percentages, hydration, levain, flour blend, scaling, dough temperature, and timelines.

Records

Keep recipes, starters, and saved plans close to the actual work.
